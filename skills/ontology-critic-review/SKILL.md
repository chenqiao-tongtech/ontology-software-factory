# Skill: independent ontology critique

- Check for duplicate or overlapping terms, ambiguous labels, missing definitions, and unsupported edges.
- Verify relation endpoints and whether a relation is truly supported by quoted source evidence.
- Flag constraints that are actually policy/authorization rules and must not be enforced by ontology alone.
- Identify missing competency questions and conflicts across source versions.
- Return findings with severity, affected IDs, rationale, and recommended follow-up.
- Do not edit, approve, or publish the candidate; the human domain owner and deterministic promotion workflow own that decision.
